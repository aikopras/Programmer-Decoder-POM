CV values used by the safety decoder

The safety (watchdog) decoder has DecType (CV27) 128. In addition to the CVs common to all decoders, the new safety decoder uses the CVs below. The old safety decoder uses other CVs, which are not described here.

CVNameAccessRangeDescription
33SendButtonFBR/W0..1If 1: the decoder sends feedback via the RS-Bus if the emergency button is pushed
34P_EmergencyR/W1..4Pin of connector X8 for the emergency stop button
35T_WatchdogR/W0..255Maximum time (in seconds) between watchdog messages; this is the time the watchdog relay remains active
36T_EmergencyR/W0..255Time (in 100 ms steps) the PC gets to stop all trains, after the emergency button is pushed
37T_CheckMoveR/W0..255Interval (in 100 ms steps) in which is checked if the PC stopped all trains
38..41T_RS_Push1 .. T_RS_Push4R/W0..255Time (in 20 ms steps) the RS-Bus stays ON after push button 1..4 is pushed. 0 = toggle button
