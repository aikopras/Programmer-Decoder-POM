CV values used by the relays-16 decoder

The relays-16 decoder has DecType (CV27) 33. In addition to the CVs common to all decoders, this decoder uses the CVs below.

CVNameAccessRangeDescription
33RactR/W0..1Relays switch with - (0) or with + (1)
34RRR1R/W0..255Relays 1-8 that are used for round-robin
35RRR2R/W0..255Relays 9-16 that are used for round-robin
36RInterR/W0..255Round-robin interval, in seconds
37ModeR/W0..30 = relays switch on an ACTIVATE command, 1 = same as 0, but a DEACTIVATE command releases the relays, 2 = multiple relays may be activated at the same time, 3 = relays are activated in a round-robin fashion
